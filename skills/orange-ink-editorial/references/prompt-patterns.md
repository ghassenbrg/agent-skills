# Prompt patterns

These are reusable blocks. Adapt them rather than copying mechanically.

## Global style lock

> Render in the established orange ink editorial visual language: warm cream/off-white base, thick hand-drawn charcoal-black brush lines, vivid orange accent strokes, occasional soft golden-yellow highlight texture, friendly editorial illustration, simple confident shapes, playful but restrained doodles, and a slightly imperfect marker/paint texture. Keep it mostly 2D and human-made in feel. Avoid anime styling, glossy 3D mascot rendering, generic corporate vectors, neon cyberpunk, photorealism, and excessive visual clutter.

## Technical elements block

> Use only a few simple hand-drawn technical symbols relevant to the topic, such as code brackets, a terminal window, cloud, database, server stack, network nodes, gear, AI chip, or arrows between components. Keep them iconic and easy to understand, not diagram-heavy.

## Doodle mascot identity block

> Preserve the reference mascot as the same minimal round-headed black brush-line mascot: white circular face, thick black outline, simple limbs, open friendly smile with orange accent, expressive eyes/wink, warm curious energy. Do not redesign him into a robot, anime character, or detailed human.

## Personal avatar identity block

> Preserve the same human avatar identity and outfit from the reference: short dark hair, black-and-white illustrated face, confident friendly expression, black open-collar jacket over a light shirt, thick ink/brush contours. Keep facial proportions stable. Do not replace the outfit with a hoodie or generic tech-bro styling unless requested.

## Professional LinkedIn block

> Make the subject look capable and approachable, not theatrical. Use a confident editorial pose such as arms crossed, relaxed three-quarter stance, holding a laptop/notebook, or a subtle presenting gesture. Keep visual energy in the background doodles rather than the body pose.

## Simplicity block

> The image should read in under two seconds. Give the face/headline clear dominance, generous negative space, and only 3–6 supporting elements. Remove anything that does not strengthen the idea.
