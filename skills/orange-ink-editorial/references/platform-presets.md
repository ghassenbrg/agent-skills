# Platform presets

## Instagram feed
- Default aspect: 4:5
- Character large and central
- Minimal text
- Strong first-screen readability
- 3–5 supporting doodles/props maximum

## Instagram profile image
- Aspect: 1:1
- Face/head should occupy most of the circle-safe center
- No small text
- Very few decorations
- High contrast at thumbnail size

## Instagram Story / Reel cover
- Aspect: 9:16
- Keep important face/text away from extreme top and bottom UI zones
- One short headline maximum

## LinkedIn personal cover
- Composition: wide 4:1
- Keep primary text near center/upper-center
- Leave the lower-left region visually calm because LinkedIn profile UI may overlap it
- Put the illustrated person toward the right third when possible
- Professional pose, restrained doodles
- Suitable content: role, specialty keywords, concise motto

## YouTube / video thumbnail
- Aspect: 16:9
- One large subject + one short idea
- Technical iconography should be bold and minimal
- High contrast and readable at small size

## Website/presentation hero
- Aspect: 16:9 unless specified
- More negative space than social posts
- Decorative details may extend into background edges
